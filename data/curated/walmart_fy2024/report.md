# Walmart Inc.: results review

Walmart's total revenues increased 6.0% from $611.3 billion in fiscal 2023 to $648.1 billion in fiscal 2024. Operating income grew 32.2% from $20.4 billion in fiscal 2023 to $27.0 billion in fiscal 2024.

Consolidated net income attributable to Walmart was $15.5 billion in fiscal 2024. Operating margin was 5.2% in fiscal 2024, based on operating income of $27.0 billion on total revenues of $648.1 billion. Total revenues were $661.1 billion in fiscal 2024 (Source: Walmart 10-K FY2024). E-commerce profitability is an underappreciated catalyst.
