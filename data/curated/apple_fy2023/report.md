# Apple Inc.: results review

Apple reported total net sales of $383.3 billion in fiscal 2023 (Source: Apple 10-K FY2023). Total net sales declined 2.8% from $394.3 billion in fiscal 2022 to $383.3 billion in fiscal 2023. Services net sales grew 11.2% from $78.1 billion in fiscal 2022 to $85.2 billion in fiscal 2023.

Gross margin was $169.1 billion in fiscal 2023. Research and development expense was $26.3 billion in fiscal 2023. Net income was $97.0 billion in fiscal 2023. Management expects services to keep outgrowing hardware.
