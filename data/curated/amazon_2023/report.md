# Amazon.com, Inc.: results review

Amazon's total net sales rose 11.8% from $514.0 billion in 2022 to $574.8 billion in 2023. Operating income was $36.9 billion in 2023, up from $12.2 billion in 2022.

Amazon posted net income of $30.4 billion in 2023 after a net loss of $2.7 billion in 2022. Operating margin was 7.4% in 2023, based on operating income of $36.9 billion and total net sales of $574.8 billion. Total net sales were $574.8 billion in 2023 (Source: Amazon 10-K 2023). Margin expansion should continue as fulfillment costs normalize.
