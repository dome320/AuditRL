# Tesla, Inc.: results review

Tesla's total revenues increased 18.8% from $81.5 billion in 2022 to $96.8 billion in 2023. Income from operations fell 34.9% from $13.7 billion in 2022 to $8.9 billion in 2023.

Operating margin was 12.2% in 2023, based on income from operations of $8.9 billion and total revenues of $96.8 billion. Net income attributable to common stockholders was $15.0 billion in 2023. Price cuts may weigh on automotive margins.
