# The Coca-Cola Company: results review

Coca-Cola's net operating revenues grew 6.4% from $43.0 billion in 2022 to $45.8 billion in 2023. Operating income was $11.3 billion in 2023, an operating margin of 24.7% on net operating revenues of $45.8 billion.

Net income attributable to shareowners was $10.7 billion in 2023 (Source: Coca-Cola 10-K 2022). Operating income rose 3.7% from $10.9 billion in 2022 to $11.3 billion in 2023. Net operating revenues were $43.0 billion in 2023. Pricing power has offset volume softness.
