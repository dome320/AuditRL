# Microsoft Corporation: results review

Microsoft's total revenue rose 6.9% from $198.3 billion in fiscal 2022 to $211.9 billion in fiscal 2023. Operating income reached $88.5 billion in fiscal 2023, an operating margin of 41.8% on revenue of $211.9 billion.

Net income was $72.4 billion in fiscal 2023 (Source: Microsoft 10-K FY2023). Gross margin was $164.1 billion in fiscal 2023. Operating income was $83.4 billion in fiscal 2023. Cloud demand remains the key driver of the investment thesis.
