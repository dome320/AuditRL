# Alphabet Inc.: results review

Alphabet's revenues increased 8.7% from $282.8 billion in 2022 to $307.4 billion in 2023. Operating margin expanded to 27.4% in 2023, with operating income of $84.3 billion on revenues of $307.4 billion.

Net income grew 18.9% from $60.0 billion in 2022 to $73.8 billion in 2023. Net income was $73.8 billion in 2023 (Source: Alphabet 10-K 2022). Revenues were $282.8 billion in 2022 (Source: Alphabet 10-K 2022). Search remains resilient despite new competition.
