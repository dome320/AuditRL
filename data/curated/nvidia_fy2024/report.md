# NVIDIA Corporation: results review

NVIDIA's revenue grew 126% from $27.0 billion in fiscal 2023 to $60.9 billion in fiscal 2024. Gross margin was 72.7% in fiscal 2024, on gross profit of $44.3 billion and revenue of $60.9 billion.

Data Center revenue was $47.5 billion in fiscal 2024. Net income rose to $29.8 million in fiscal 2024. Operating income was $32.0 billion in fiscal 2024. We believe supply constraints could ease over the coming year.
